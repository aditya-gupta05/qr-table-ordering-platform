# Lantern Kitchen: Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Lantern Kitchen, a neighbourhood restaurant at 88 Harbour Street serving wood-fired and wok-cooked food from across Asia and the Mediterranean. This page answers the questions our team hears most often. If your question is not here, ask any member of staff or call us on (555) 014-2290.

## Opening hours and bookings

### What are your opening hours?
We are open seven days a week. Monday to Thursday we serve from 11:30 to 22:00, Friday and Saturday from 11:30 to 23:00, and Sunday from 12:00 to 21:00. These are the hours the doors are open; the kitchen takes its final orders thirty minutes before closing, so the last food order is at 21:30 Monday to Thursday, 22:30 on Friday and Saturday, and 20:30 on Sunday. Drinks and desserts can usually still be ordered until closing time.

### Do you open on public holidays?
We are open on most public holidays with the same hours as a Sunday. We close on Christmas Day and on New Year's Day. Holiday hours are posted on the front door and on our website at least two weeks ahead, and they are also read out by the voicemail on our phone line.

### Do I need a reservation?
No. Walk-ins are always welcome and we keep about half of our twelve tables free for them. Friday and Saturday evenings between 18:00 and 20:30 are our busiest times, and a walk-in party of four or more may wait twenty to forty minutes then. Reservations are strongly recommended for those slots and for any group of six or more.

### How do I make, change or cancel a reservation?
You can book through our website or by phoning (555) 014-2290 between 10:00 and 21:00. To change or cancel, call us or reply to your confirmation email. We hold a table for fifteen minutes after the booked time; after that it may be given to walk-in guests. Parties of eight or more have a stricter policy, described on the policies page.

### What is the largest group you can seat?
We can seat up to fourteen guests by joining tables, and our back room (the Lantern Room) holds up to twenty for private hire. Groups of eight or more are treated as large parties and are subject to a card guarantee and an automatic gratuity. Please see the large party section of our policies for the details.

### Can I request a specific table?
You may ask for a corner table, a quieter table, or a table by the window when you book, and we will do our best, but we cannot guarantee a specific table. Tables are numbered T1 to T12; the QR code on each table links your phone to that table number so your order reaches the kitchen with the right label. Tables T3 and T7 have removable chairs for wheelchair users.

### Can I book the whole restaurant?
Yes, on Sunday evenings and on Monday and Tuesday evenings after 18:00. A full buyout seats forty-six guests and requires a set menu chosen in advance. Contact hello@lanternkitchen.example with your date, headcount and any dietary needs, and our events manager will reply within two working days.

## Ordering with the QR code

### How does ordering from my phone work?
Each table has a small QR code. Scan it with your phone camera, browse the menu, add dishes to your basket, and press the order button. Your order appears on the kitchen screen within a fraction of a second, and the status on your phone moves from received to preparing to ready as the kitchen works on it. You do not need to download an app or create an account.

### Can I still order from a waiter?
Of course. QR ordering is a convenience, not a requirement. If you prefer to order from a person, or you have questions about ingredients, wave to any member of staff. Staff can also add dishes to your table's order on a handheld device.

### Can I order drinks and food separately, and order more later?
Yes. Every time you press the order button it creates a new order for your table, so you can start with a drink, add starters ten minutes later and dessert at the end. Orders are not merged, so each one is sent to the kitchen as it is placed.

### Can I cancel or change an order after I place it?
Within about a minute, while the status still says received, ask a member of staff and we will remove it. Once the status is preparing, the kitchen has started cooking and we cannot cancel it, though we will always try to fix a genuine mistake. Please do not place the same order twice if your connection is slow; the system treats repeated taps as one order, but a second order sent after a refresh will be a new one.

### Does QR ordering take payment?
No. The QR system only sends your order to the kitchen. You pay at the end of the meal by asking your server, who brings a card machine to the table. We accept all major credit and debit cards, contactless payments and cash. We do not accept cheques.

### Can we split the bill?
Yes, evenly or by item, up to six ways. Please tell your server before the card machine comes to the table. Because orders from the QR system are grouped by table, itemised splits take a moment longer, so thank you for your patience.

## Families, children and celebrations

### Do you have a children's menu?
We do not print a separate children's menu. Instead, half portions are available on request for Margherita Pizza, Shoestring Fries, Steamed Jasmine Rice, Mushroom Risotto and Mango Sorbet at sixty per cent of the menu price. Staff will add these to your order manually, since the QR menu shows full portions only. Plain rice and fries are also welcome additions for fussy eaters.

### Do you have high chairs and booster seats?
We have four high chairs and four booster seats. They are first come, first served, but if you mention a baby or toddler when you book we will set one aside. There is a baby-changing shelf in the accessible washroom at the back of the dining room.

### Can I bring a birthday cake?
Yes. We are happy to keep a cake in our fridge and bring it out with candles at the time you choose. There is a cake service charge of three dollars per guest, which covers plates, cutlery and slicing. If you would rather have a dessert from our menu with a candle, there is no charge for the candle.

### Do you decorate for celebrations?
For birthdays and anniversaries we can place a small sign and a lantern on the table if you let us know when booking. Balloons and confetti are not allowed because they interfere with our other guests and the wood-fired oven. Large private events in the Lantern Room can bring their own decorations as long as nothing is fixed to the walls.

## Getting here

### Where do I park?
We do not have our own car park. Metered street parking on Harbour Street is free after 18:00 and on Sundays. The Harbour Car Park, a two-minute walk away on Pier Lane, charges a flat evening rate of six dollars after 17:00. We cannot validate parking tickets. There are bicycle racks directly outside the front door.

### Are you accessible by public transport?
Yes. The number 14 and number 22 buses stop at Harbour Street, about fifty metres from the door. The nearest rail station, Quayside, is a ten-minute walk. Taxis and ride-share drivers can stop outside the front door for drop-offs.

### Is the restaurant step-free?
Yes. The main entrance is level with the pavement, the dining room is on one floor, and the accessible washroom is at the back of the room. Full details are in our accessibility policy.

## Takeaway, gift cards and extras

### Do you do takeaway?
Yes. Phone us on (555) 014-2290 or order at the counter. Takeaway orders normally take twenty to twenty-five minutes. Everything leaves in compostable containers. Items that go soggy, such as fried dishes, travel better if you eat them within fifteen minutes. QR ordering from the table is for dining in only and cannot be used for takeaway.

### Do you deliver?
No. We do not offer delivery ourselves and we do not list on third-party delivery apps. We would rather your dinner arrive hot than late.

### Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, in amounts from twenty-five dollars in increments of five dollars, at the counter or on our website. Gift cards are valid for three years from the purchase date, can be used for food and drink in the restaurant, and cannot be exchanged for cash. A lost card can be replaced if you have the receipt.

### Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes, a free guest network called LanternGuest. The password changes weekly and is written on the small chalkboard by the host stand; staff will share it if you ask. Please avoid large downloads so the connection stays quick for the kitchen systems.

### Is there a dress code?
No. Come as you are. The dining room is warm because of the wood-fired oven, so lighter clothes are comfortable in summer.

### Is there background music?
We play soft instrumental music until 20:00 and a livelier playlist on Friday and Saturday nights. If you would like it quieter or turned down, just ask.

### Can I bring my own food?
Outside food is not allowed, except baby food and a birthday cake as described above. Guests may bring their own wine; our corkage terms are on the policies page.

### I lost something at the restaurant. What should I do?
Phone us or email hello@lanternkitchen.example with a description. We keep lost property in the office for thirty days, then donate clothing to a local charity and discard anything else.

## Dietary needs

### Do you cater for vegetarians and vegans?
Yes. Each dish on the QR menu shows dietary tags such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten free, halal and dairy free, and you can filter the menu by tag. Our kitchen cooks meat and fish, so vegetarian and vegan dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen with dedicated utensils where possible. Our practices page explains what vegan and vegetarian mean to us.

### I have a food allergy. What should I do?
Tell us before you order, even if you are using the QR menu. The menu lists the allergens that are ingredients of each dish, and you can filter out the allergens you avoid, but our kitchen handles all the major allergens and we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment. Please read our allergen and kitchen practices page and talk to a member of staff, who will check with the chef.

### Can the chatbot confirm that a dish is safe for my allergy?
The assistant on the ordering page answers from our menu data and our written policies. For allergies it will tell you what the menu lists and, if it is not certain, it will offer to call a waiter. It will never promise that a dish is safe. A member of the kitchen team is the final authority.

### Do you offer gluten-free options?
Several dishes are marked gluten free. Our shoestring fries are cooked in a dedicated gluten-free fryer. Other gluten-free dishes are prepared in a kitchen that also handles wheat, so people with coeliac disease should read the kitchen practices page and speak with staff.

### Is your food halal?
Dishes tagged halal use halal-certified meat and no alcohol in the cooking. We also cook pork in the same kitchen, with separate utensils, so we are not a fully halal-certified restaurant. See the practices page for specifics.

### Which dishes are spicy?
Every dish shows a spice level from zero to three chillies. Level one is a gentle warmth, level two is clearly spicy, and level three is hot. Our Thai Green Curry and Spicy Tofu Stir-Fry are the spiciest dishes on the menu. If a dish is too hot, the kitchen can send out extra rice, cucumber or a mango lassi.

## Prices, payment and service

### Are the prices on the QR menu the final prices?
Yes. The prices shown are in US dollars and include sales tax. The total on your phone is calculated by our server from the current menu, not from your device, so it always matches what is on your bill. If a price changes while you are browsing, the total shown after you order is the one that counts.

### Is service charge included?
Not for parties under eight. For smaller tables, gratuity is at your discretion and most guests leave between fifteen and twenty per cent. Parties of eight or more have an automatic eighteen per cent gratuity added. Tips go entirely to the floor and kitchen team and are shared by hours worked.

### Can I pay with a foreign card or currency?
Foreign cards work on our card machine. We only accept US dollars in cash and we cannot give change in other currencies.

### What if I am unhappy with a dish?
Tell us straight away. If something is not cooked as the menu describes or does not taste right, we will remake it or take it off the bill without any argument. Please do not wait until the end of the meal, because we cannot fix a dish we do not know about.

### Is the restaurant suitable for a business lunch?
Yes. The best time is between 11:30 and 14:30 on weekdays, when our set lunch is served and tables turn faster. Ask for a table in the back half of the room, which is quieter, and tell us if you need receipts split between companies. Details of the set lunch are on the printed menu.

### How long does the kitchen usually take?
Starters and sides normally reach the table within eight to twelve minutes of your order. Mains take fifteen to twenty-five minutes, and wood-fired pizzas arrive in about twelve. During the Friday and Saturday evening rush add ten minutes. If your order has been waiting longer than that, wave to a member of staff, who can check on its status.

### Do you have a smoking or vaping area?
Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere inside or on the patio. Guests who smoke can use the pavement beyond the bicycle racks, away from the door.

### Is there a patio?
Yes, a patio with eight tables that is open from May to September, weather permitting. Patio tables are not reservable and are first come, first served. Dogs on a lead are welcome on the patio only.
