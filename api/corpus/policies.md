# Lantern Kitchen: House Policies

This document sets out the formal policies that apply to every guest at Lantern Kitchen, 88 Harbour Street. They are written in full so that there is no ambiguity at the table. If a situation is not covered here, the duty manager will make a fair decision and, where it is reasonable to do so, in your favour.

## Large party bookings

A large party is any booking of eight guests or more. Because a large party takes up several of our twelve tables and a disproportionate share of the kitchen's capacity for about two hours, we handle these bookings differently from smaller ones. The maximum party we will seat in the main dining room is fourteen; larger groups must use the Lantern Room, which holds up to twenty guests and has its own minimum spend. To confirm a large party booking we take a card number as a guarantee. The card is not charged unless the party cancels late or does not arrive, as described in the cancellation policy below. We ask the organiser to confirm the final headcount forty-eight hours before the booking, and we hold the table for the confirmed number only.

For large parties we strongly encourage a pre-order. Sending us the main dishes at least a day ahead lets the kitchen stagger the wood-fired oven and the wok station so that your table is served together rather than in waves. Pre-orders are not compulsory, but when a party of eight or more arrives without one, starters are limited to a shared selection of up to four dishes and mains are taken from the QR menu in a single round. Dietary requirements and allergies must be given at the time of booking, not on the evening, because the chef needs to check ingredients and prepare separate utensils in advance. Every member of the party who has an allergy must tell their server on arrival even if the organiser has already told us, so that the information is attached to the right person.

Large parties are given a maximum table time of two and a half hours on Friday and Saturday evenings and three hours at all other times. If another booking follows yours we will tell you in advance. On most nights we can offer you another seating area at the bar to continue your conversation once the table is needed.

## Gratuity and service charge

Gratuity is not included in the bill for parties of seven guests or fewer. For these tables, gratuity is entirely discretionary, and our guests commonly leave between fifteen and twenty per cent of the pre-tax subtotal. For large parties of eight or more, an automatic gratuity of eighteen per cent of the pre-tax subtotal is added to the bill and shown as a separate line. We apply this automatic gratuity because large parties are served by several team members at once, and it ensures that the whole team is paid fairly for that work. The gratuity on a large party bill can be adjusted or removed by the duty manager if the service did not meet our standards; please tell the manager during the meal if you feel this applies, and do not wait until you have paid.

All gratuities, both automatic and discretionary, go to the team and not to the restaurant. They are pooled each week and divided among the floor and kitchen staff in proportion to hours worked. We do not deduct card processing fees from gratuities. A guest who wants to leave a tip for a specific person can do so in cash and say so, and that tip is kept by that person.

## Corkage

Guests are welcome to bring wine to Lantern Kitchen for a corkage fee of twenty-five dollars per 750 ml bottle. A table may bring a maximum of two bottles, and a magnum counts as two bottles. Corkage applies to wine and sparkling wine only; spirits, beer and cocktails may not be brought in. We ask that you do not bring a wine that we sell. Our list is short, so this will rarely matter: the house Pinot Grigio, served by the glass for eleven dollars, is the only wine we pour, and so bringing that same wine is not allowed. Our team will open and serve your bottle, provide glassware and decant if you ask. If you also buy two or more glasses of our house wine, the corkage fee on your first bottle is waived.

Corkage is not available for the Lantern Room on private hire evenings unless it has been agreed at the time of the booking. The corkage fee is charged even if a bottle is brought in and left unopened, because the service has already been prepared. We reserve the right to decline to serve a bottle that appears to be counterfeit, spoiled or unsafe to drink.

## Cancellations and no-shows

For bookings of up to seven guests, we ask for as much notice as you can give, but there is no fee for cancelling or changing your booking, and no fee for a no-show on a first occasion. If a guest has missed two bookings without notice, we may ask for a card guarantee on future reservations.

For large parties of eight or more the policy is firmer. A cancellation or a reduction of more than two guests made more than twenty-four hours before the booking is free. A cancellation made within twenty-four hours, or a no-show, will be charged at fifteen dollars per guest on the card held as guarantee, up to the original number confirmed forty-eight hours before the booking. This charge compensates for the food ordered, the tables held empty, and the walk-in guests we turned away. We will waive it in cases of genuine emergency, illness or severe weather that affects travel; ask to speak to the manager.

If the restaurant must cancel your booking, for example because of a kitchen equipment failure or a power cut, we will contact you as early as we can, offer you the next available date, and refund any deposit in full.

## Accessibility

Lantern Kitchen is committed to welcoming guests of every ability. The main entrance is level with the pavement and has a push-button door opener. The dining room is on a single floor, with at least 90 centimetres between tables along the main route, and the accessible washroom at the back of the dining room has a wide door, grab rails and an emergency cord. Tables T3 and T7 have chairs that can be removed, which allows a wheelchair user to sit at the table rather than at its end. Please tell us when you book if you would like one of these tables; otherwise they are released to walk-in guests after 19:00.

Menus can be provided in large print on request. The QR menu on your phone supports your device's text-size and screen-reader settings, and it can be navigated without a mouse or touch if you use an assistive keyboard. Our staff are trained to describe dishes aloud and to read the menu to guests with low vision. Guests who are deaf or hard of hearing are welcome to type a question to a member of staff on their phone or on a small whiteboard that we keep at the host stand. We switch off the background music at your request. We are not able to provide a hearing loop, though we are looking into one.

If you need assistance, for example help carrying a tray, cutting food, or reaching the washroom, please ask. We would much rather help than have you struggle.

## Service animals and pets

Service animals are welcome throughout the restaurant, including the dining room and the kitchen pass, and are never charged for. Under the law, we may ask only two questions of a person with a service animal: whether the animal is required because of a disability, and what work or task it has been trained to perform. We do not ask for documentation or a demonstration. A service animal must be under the control of its handler, on a lead or harness, and we ask that it stay out of the aisles so that servers carrying hot dishes can pass.

Emotional-support animals and pets are not treated as service animals. Pets on a lead may sit with their owners on the patio, which is open from May to September, and our team will bring them a bowl of water. Pets may not enter the indoor dining room. If another guest has an allergy to animals and raises a concern, the manager will find both parties a comfortable arrangement, which may include moving the pet's table.

## Behaviour and conduct

We want every guest to enjoy their evening. Guests who are abusive to staff or other guests, who damage property, or who are visibly intoxicated may be asked to leave, and the bill for what has been served up to that point will still be due. We serve alcohol only to guests aged eighteen or over and may ask for photographic identification from anyone who appears to be under twenty-five. Children must stay with an adult at all times and may not be left unattended at a table. Use of mobile phones for calls is welcome only at the bar and on the pavement outside; please step away from the table for long calls so that your neighbours can enjoy their meals. Filming other guests or the kitchen without permission is not allowed.

## Refunds and complaints

If a dish is wrongly prepared, arrives cold, or is not as described, tell a member of staff and it will be replaced or removed from the bill. Complaints raised after payment are handled in writing: email hello@lanternkitchen.example within seven days with the date, table number and a description of the problem. A manager will reply within three working days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method; cash payments are refunded in cash only if the guest returns with the receipt. We do not refund service charges on large party bills after payment has been made unless the duty manager agreed during the meal. Orders made through the QR system and shown on your receipt can be disputed in the same way; the order number and time on your phone are stored with the bill.

## Privacy and the ordering system

When you scan the table QR code, we record only your table number and the items you order. The ordering system does not ask for your name, email address or phone number and does not place tracking cookies on your device. Questions you type into the assistant on the ordering page are processed to give you an answer and may be stored in anonymised form to improve our menu information; they are never sold and are never linked to your payment details. If you want any record of your questions deleted, ask a member of staff or email us with the time of your visit.

## Private hire of the Lantern Room

The Lantern Room is a separate room at the back of the restaurant with its own door, its own sound system and seating for up to twenty guests at a long table or twelve at a round table. It can be booked for lunches, dinners and receptions. The minimum spend is six hundred dollars on Monday to Thursday and nine hundred dollars on Friday to Sunday, before gratuity. A deposit of twenty-five per cent of the minimum spend is taken at the time of booking and deducted from the final bill. The deposit is refundable if the booking is cancelled more than fourteen days ahead, half refundable between fourteen and seven days ahead, and not refundable within seven days. Menus for the Lantern Room are agreed at least five days ahead and must list every guest's allergy and dietary requirement. The automatic eighteen per cent gratuity applies to all Lantern Room events. Decorations may not be fixed to walls, and the room must be vacated thirty minutes after the agreed end time.
