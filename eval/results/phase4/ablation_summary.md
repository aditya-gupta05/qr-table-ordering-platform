## 4. Abstention signal (dev)

Rule: answer when the signal >= threshold, otherwise say 'I don't know'. Signal = top-1 RRF score (`final`, hybrid), best raw cosine among the returned hits (`cosine`), or the cross-encoder score of the best hit (`rerank`, rerank run). Threshold chosen to maximise balanced accuracy (BA) on the dev queries; `in-sample` = scored on the queries it was chosen on, `LOO` = leave-one-out (threshold re-chosen without the held-out query).
